# NAIJA HUSTLE: Story

> Twelve story missions, all set in Lagos, built on the cast in [GDD.md](GDD.md). Everyone and everything here is fictional: no real
> banks, groups, politicians, companies or events. Missions describe what the player does in the game, not
> real-world methods.

## Logline

Two friends from the Lagos mainland, a danfo conductor and a phone-repair genius, go from collecting fares to
robbing banks to pay off a dead man's debt. Along the way they cross a campus cult, a con, a kidnapping and an
election. The man at the top of it all wants one of them to pull one last trigger.

## The two leads (switch between them, GTA-style)

| | **Tunde Adeyemi, 24** | **Amaka Nwosu, 23** |
|---|---|---|
| From | Oshoja Junction | Balo Market |
| Who | Danfo conductor. Quick mouth, quick feet, loyal, takes the hits. | Phone-repair and hacking genius. Sharp, funny, fearless. |
| Plays best at | Driving, chases, fights, the heavy jobs | Stealth, cons, hacking phones and cameras, the getaway plan |
| Wants | To clear his late father's debt and get the family bus back | To get her little brother Chidi out of the cult, and to get out of the market for good |
| Special ability | **Hustle Rush:** a burst of speed and toughness in chases and fights | **Unlock:** hacks phones, CCTV and traffic lights within range |

- **The debt:** Tunde's father Femi drove a danfo until **Shina Garage**, the motor-park strongman, seized it over
  an unpaid "levy". Femi died owing ₦350,000, and Tunde's mother **Iya Tobi** inherited the debt.
- **The love story:** Tunde and Amaka grew up together. It grows through the missions, and the endings decide
  whether they end up together.
- **Switching:** press the switch button in free roam to jump to the other lead wherever they are. Some missions
  are played as one lead, some switch between both mid-mission (the heist and the finale).

The **Integrity** stat and three choices decide the ending.

## Endings

| Ending | When | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **The Broadcast** (best) | Zainab lives (Choice 2: fake it or fight), you kept the ledger (Choice 3), Integrity ≥ +30 | The ledger airs live. The Kingmaker withdraws, Big Bar's accounts are frozen and Shina is arrested. Epilogue at Oshoja Park: no levy, Iya Tobi owns Femi's danfo, and Tunde and Amaka run it together. |
| **The Big Man's Boy** | You took the shot (Choice 2), or Integrity ≤ −30 | Tunde becomes Big Bar's enforcer, rich and untouchable. Amaka leaves him. Epilogue: his convoy sweeps past Oshoja, where a new boy collects the levy for him. |
| **Gone Quiet** | Anything else | Tunde and Amaka take the Crestline money and lie low in Stilt Town Waters. The ledger never airs, and someone at Oshoja still pays the levy. |

## How it plays

- **Every mission is a set piece** with a core verb: drive, chase, stealth, heist, defend.
- **The heist (8) has a planning phase:**
  - pick a crew role for each of Amaka, Chidi and Baba Sule
  - pick a getaway route
  - choose a loud or quiet approach

  Those choices change how the mission plays.
- **Choices** change dialogue, who helps you in the finale and the ending. Integrity moves with smaller
  choices too: returning the scam money, sparing people, paying off or fighting the agbero.
- **The phone** carries the story between missions: texts from contacts, threats, and *Yarns* reacting to your
  viral moments.
- **Violence stays in the game's world:** fictional groups, no real-world methods, and consequences for the
  player (wanted level, Integrity, who trusts you).

## What's playable today

Mission 1 (**First Day on the Danfo**) is playable in the browser demo and in the Unreal plugin. The browser
demo's other missions become parts of these: Balo Market Run feeds mission 2, Go-Slow Getaway mission 4 and
Third Lagoon Bridge mission 11. Night Bus East is retired for now, since the story stays in Lagos.
