# Music in NAIJA HUSTLE

Every track the game plays is listed here with where it came from and the right the game has to use it. The rule
(audio brief, section 6): only original commissions, royalty-free libraries whose licence allows games, or AI music
services whose terms allow commercial use. No real songs or samples without a licence.

**No audio file is in this repo.** Tracks live on the developer's machine under `~/Downloads/nh-radio/` and are
brought into the project by `Scripts/import_radio.py`; the playlists are in `Data/radio_stations.json`.
