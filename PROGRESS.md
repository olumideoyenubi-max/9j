# NAIJA HUSTLE (Unreal) progress

**Last updated:** 2026-10-09 (audio brief step 1: the mix; radio station Ragebait FM; car stealing, roll and climb, directions; phone and DropAm; real-scale Lagos level; before that: player built to the brief with MakeHuman; driver in the seat; headlights; real models on 15 of 16 vehicle types)
**Engine:** Unreal Engine 5.8, macOS, Metal SM5
**Test machine:** Apple M1, 8 GB RAM

## Latest frame rate

Standalone game, 1280×720 window, standing on the dusty street (8000, 14000, facing west), 15-second average.

| Lighting | Frame rate | Frame time |
|---|---|---|
| Harsh morning, 9:30 | 22.9 fps | 43.7 ms |
| Golden evening, 17:00 | 23.5 fps | 42.5 ms |
| Harsh morning, 9:30, repo project with `Config/Mac/MacEngine.ini` | 24.8 fps | 40.3 ms |

The first two rows were measured in a UE 5.8 template project with its default config (Lumen switched off at run time by the lighting rig). The third is this repo's own project with the Mac profile; the picture looks the same.

The target is 30 fps at 720p–900p; the game is not there yet.

## Done

- **Plugin on UE 5.8:** `NaijaHustleGame` compiles and runs on the Mac, inside a UE 5.8 Third Person template project. One API fix (JSON map keys).
- **8 GB memory fix:** on machines with 8 GB or less the lighting rig turns off Lumen GI, volumetric fog and clouds, and uses screen-space reflections. This stopped a Metal out-of-memory crash on Play.
- **Look pass 1:** surface-type material pipeline (`M_NHSurface` and one `MI_NHSurface_<Type>` per surface), overhead cables, ruts and rubbish on dusty streets, harsh-morning preset, over-the-shoulder sprint camera.
- **Playtest tooling:** debug commands `NHGoto`, `NHBoard`, `NHAgbero`, `NHFinish`, `NHAutoplay`, `NHSelfTest`. Autoplay passes 26 checks and the self-test has 75 checks, with no plugin warnings.
- **Luxury cars (blockouts):** eight Unreal-only vehicle types with made-up names and 16 parked spots (a second super SUV at Oshodi Motor Park, bay 12). Loader for real models is in place (`assign_vehicle_meshes.py`).
- **Road look:** worn grey asphalt, faded markings, black-and-white kerb stones.
- **Visual brief, section 1 (lighting):** dynamic lighting with faked bounce from the sky light, ambient occlusion, a second haze layer, sun light-shaft bloom, a golden-evening preset (16:00–17:30), an F1 lighting menu, and 54 reflection captures over road junctions.

Nine original sounds made in code (`NHWeaponSounds`: shots, their echoes off the street, bullet knock, swing, steel on something hard, drawing and putting away), three takes each. A muzzle flash lights the street. **Nothing is hurt and nobody reacts: there is no damage, ammunition, aiming or animation yet.** Checked by a recorded run (`-NHWeaponTest`); clip in `docs/clips/`.
- **Weapons do damage (2026-10-09):** bullets and the machete hurt people (two or three hits put one down), everybody near a gunshot runs, vehicles lose health and can be wrecked, and shooting earns wanted stars. Baba Driver cannot be hurt. Checked by `-NHDamageTest` (6 hits, 2 down, 10 running, 5 stars from two seconds of AK-47 into a dozen passers-by). **Not built:** anyone fighting back, police arriving, drivers reacting when their car is shot, the player being hurt. Shooting a vehicle was not exercised by the test.
- **Who comes for you (2026-10-09):** wanted stars now bring somebody (`ANHResponse`, `docs/RESPONSE.md`). On ordinary streets the Task Force come at 2 stars within 1.5 km of a station and at 3 within 4 km; on area boys' streets no Task Force comes and the area boys do, with machetes; on high-class streets the Task Force come only once a witness has lived six seconds to call them. The player has health (a bar bottom left); at nothing they are arrested and bailed or beaten and robbed, and start again where the day began. Areas and stations are data (`Data/response_areas.json`). Checked by `-NHResponseTest` in both levels. **The witness call has not been played, and they only come on foot.**
- **People are cast by their part (2026-10-09):** who a person is now follows what they do (`ENHCast`). Whoever comes for the player (area boys, the Task Force, a car owner who gives chase) is always a man, and one of the Nigerian men (Tunde, Dayo, Emeka) in turn. Baba Driver is the oldest man the project has and walks slowly. Passengers and passers-by are still anyone. **Baba is not yet elderly to look at:** the oldest body is middle-aged Emeka with black hair; a grey-haired old man needs a new MakeHuman body named "Baba", which the game will use as soon as it exists.
- **An elderly Baba Driver, and area boys dressed for it (2026-10-09):** Baba is a new MakeHuman body (`Baba` in `build_people_makehuman.py`: the oldest age, grey hair, beard and brows) in a loose shirt, plain trousers and slippers; the game casts him wherever a part needs an elderly man. Area boys wear a T-shirt or singlet, trousers or shorts, and slippers; the Task Force a black shirt, cargo trousers and boots (`ANHPerson::Dress`). Seen in the game (`docs/screenshots/baba-elder.jpg`) and in the Oshodi test's log. His skin is still the middle-aged one: the packs have no old skin.
- **Living-Lagos brief, step 1: storage baseline (2026-10-09):** `docs/STORAGE.md`. The project is 3.9 GB with 9.3 GB of disk free; characters are 1.3 GB of the 2.25 GB of content and the whole city 0.55 GB. `Scripts/storage_audit.py` measures it without Unreal; 77 district pak chunks are planned and labelled (`Data/chunks.json`, `Scripts/setup_chunks.py`) with packaging settings in `Config/DefaultGame.ini`; three more data layers are in the level script. **No packaged build has been made, so chunk sizes are uncooked figures and the packaging settings are unproven.** No size fix has been applied yet.
- **Living-Lagos brief, step 2: traffic bubble, zones, pedestrians (2026-10-09):** `docs/POPULATION.md`. Traffic follows `Data/population_zones.json` (mainland, island and expressway mixes, six bands of the day), never uses more than eight models at once, reaches further ahead at speed, and pools its vehicles instead of destroying them. `ANHCrowd` puts walkers on the pavements round the player. The Traffic setting now runs from 8+4 vehicles and 8 people to 22+10 and 25. Measured at Oshodi at rush hour: 14+8 vehicles and 16 people at 33 to 35 fps (it was 9+6 and nobody at 28), 22+10 and 25 at 31 to 36 fps, 5.3 to 5.5 GB. **Not checked: the island and expressway mixes, night, the bubble at speed.** No crowd beyond 90 m, no rust, stickers or plates, no BRT.

## Known bugs and gaps

- Wardrobe: the body's skin is hidden under clothes in 30 patches worked out from each piece's own mask; where a woman's piece needed a finer split (176 different masks into 30 patches) a small gap can show at a neckline (seen on Amaka in the halter dress), or a little skin can show through. A passer-by flagging a danfo no longer raises an arm (the real bodies have an idle and a walk only). Passers-by have no lower-detail versions for distance.

- **Frame rate with the real models (after the level-of-detail pass, 2026-10-08):** 16.5 fps at Oshodi Motor Park (was 14.9), 23.9 fps on Lagos Island (was 20.1; 29.9 with blockouts), 20 to 24 fps on the dusty street. One 15-second run each at 1280×720, on a busy Mac (load average 4 to 8), so treat differences of a few fps as noise. With every model switched back to a blockout the motor park ran at 20.0 fps, so the models now cost about 3 fps there and the rest is the scene itself: that belongs to visual brief section 9 (performance). Per-pixel lit glass was tried and dropped: it took Lagos Island down to 11 fps.
- Real-model cars keep their own colours and materials: the game's paint colours, wetness and crash marks do not show on them, and the wheels do not turn.
- Glass could not be identified on the luxury coupe, so its driver cannot be seen; the cabin view shows whatever interior a model has, which on some is very little. The driver's seat position has only been checked in the danfo, the keke and on the okada. He does not hold the wheel or wear the trainers while seated.
- Headlights have no gamepad button (every button is taken) and the lamp models do not glow; other traffic has no lights.
- The models' sizes come from fitting each to its type's length, so a few are too tall (SUV 258 cm, danfo 257 cm, luxury 4x4 226 cm).

Not exercised: finishing a hotwire by pressing E in time, the mechanic, the respray, the car keys' lock, a tracker's heat.
